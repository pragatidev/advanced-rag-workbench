# ACME privacy and retention

Customer email, phone, and national id are PII. Retrieval must redact national id before a chunk is sent to a model. Email may stay if the caller has role=support.

Do not send raw PII to a public web search. Web fallback for retrieval is off unless legal has signed the policy exception.

Themes across this corpus: sequential revenue reporting, billing integrity, least-privilege access, and PII minimization. Those four themes are the global answer. A single vector hit will not list them.
